# Indecisive 🗓️

**No Bojio!!!**

Indecisive is a group scheduling app that helps friends find a date everyone can actually make. Create a hangout, share the room code, and let everyone mark their availability. The app highlights the dates that work for the whole group so you can stop going back and forth in the group chat.

---

## What You Can Do

- Create a shared hangout room.
- Invite friends with a 6-character room code or room link.
- Pick the dates you’re available.
- Optionally mark specific times you’re **not free** on a chosen date.
- See everyone’s availability in one place.
- Find dates that work for everyone.
- Lock in the final hangout date.
- Add the final date to Google Calendar.
- Re-open date selection if plans change.

---

## Getting Started

1. Open Indecisive in your browser.
2. Sign in with your Google account.
3. Choose **Create a hangout** or **Join a hangout**.

> Google sign-in is required to create, join, or manage a hangout.

---

## Create a Hangout

1. Tap **Create a hangout**.
2. Enter your name.
3. Optionally limit everyone to a specific date range.
4. Optionally give the hangout a name, such as “Weekend BBQ” or “Beach Day”.
5. Tap **Create & pick my dates**.
6. Select the dates you’re available.
7. Optionally add busy times for individual dates.
8. Review your summary and share the room code or link with your friends.

---

## Join a Hangout

1. Tap **Join a hangout**.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter the 6-character room code from your friend.
4. Open the room.
5. Tap **Add my availability**.
6. Pick your dates and continue to the summary.

If you open a room link directly, you’ll be taken to the room after signing in.

---

## Picking Your Dates

- Tap a date to select or deselect it.
- On desktop or mobile, you can drag across multiple dates.
- Use **Weekends only** to limit selection to Saturdays and Sundays.
- Use **Clear** to remove all selected dates.
- If none of the dates work for you, choose **I’m not free on any dates**.

If the hangout has a date range, only dates inside that range can be selected.

### Adding Busy Times

For any selected date, you can optionally add a time range when you are **not free**.

- Leave both time fields blank if you’re free all day.
- Use the **×** button to clear a busy range.
- A busy range must have both a start and end time, and the end time must be after the start time.

Busy times are extra context for the group. They do not change which dates count as overlapping.

---

## Reviewing and Saving Availability

After picking dates, you’ll see a summary of:

- The dates you selected.
- Any busy time ranges you added.

Your availability is saved automatically. Once it’s synced, you can copy and share the room link.

If saving fails, use **Retry Sync**. You can also go back and edit your dates at any time.

---

## Inside a Hangout Room

The room shows:

- Everyone who has submitted availability.
- Each person’s selected dates.
- A small indicator on dates where someone has a busy time.
- A list of exact busy times below each month’s dates.
- Dates that work for **everyone**, highlighted as overlap dates.

### Locking In a Final Date

1. Find a highlighted overlap date.
2. Tap the date to select it as the final hangout date.
3. Confirm the decision.

Once confirmed, the room shows the official hangout date and an **Add to Google Calendar** button.

If plans change, tap **Re-open date selection** to clear the final date and let everyone update their availability again.

The person who created the room can also delete the hangout permanently.

---

## Recent Hangouts

Signed-in users can find their recent hangouts on the home page and reopen them quickly.

---

## Tips

- Share the **room code or room link** with everyone you want to invite.
- Make sure everyone selects the same date for it to appear as an overlap.
- Busy times are for information only — they do not automatically choose a meeting time.
- If no dates overlap, ask everyone to update their availability.
- If your Google sign-in popup is blocked, allow popups for the site and try again.

---

## FAQ

### Do I need a Google account?

Yes. Google sign-in is required to create, join, and manage hangouts.

### Why are there no overlapping dates?

A date only appears as an overlap when **every participant** has selected it.

### Can I change my availability later?

Yes. Open the room and choose **Edit / Update my availability**.

### Do busy times affect the overlap?

No. Overlap is based on selected dates. Busy times are shown for extra context.

### Does the app create a calendar event automatically?

No. After the final date is locked in, use the **Add to Google Calendar** button.

### Can someone else lock in the final date?

Yes. Any participant in the room can lock in or re-open a date.

### What happens if I clear my dates?

Your availability is saved as empty, and the room will show that you’re not available on any dates.
